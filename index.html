A decision brief, not another inbox

Make every message earn its interruption.

Comfy has many good notification surfaces. The problem is that their timing, identity, and suppression rules are mostly independent. The answer is not one universal component. It is one shared policy for when a message deserves attention.

18 surface families mapped11 PostHog surveys visible3 codebases reviewed

The recommendation

Consolidate the decision layer first. Keep specialized UI where it helps users understand, act, or learn.

One policy, many renderers.

Every message should enter through the same eligibility and suppression contract. The renderer can then be a toast, banner, coachmark, modal, email, survey, or durable documentation.

Why now? The Slack threads describe banners from PostHog, system notifications, Customer.io, and Desktop colliding in the same experience. The recent agent/partner-node overlap is the visible symptom of missing shared policy.

Use the least disruptive surface

Escalate only when the user needs to act now, or when the information will otherwise disappear.

Default

Docs

Durable reference, changelog, Help Center, What’s New.

Light touch

Banner

Broad announcement with a visible but dismissible presence.

Contextual

Toast

Transient status, success, error, or a release hint.

Guided

Coachmark

Teach a new control exactly where it lives.

Exceptional

Modal

Only for decisions, onboarding gates, or focused feedback.
